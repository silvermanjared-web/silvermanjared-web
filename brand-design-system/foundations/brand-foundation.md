# Brand Foundation

## System name

Brand Design System

## Mission

Create a consistent, flexible design foundation that helps teams move faster without sacrificing clarity, accessibility, or brand quality.

## Brand principles

### Clear before clever

The system should make choices easy. Interfaces, copy, and components should reduce ambiguity and help users understand what to do next.

### Warm but credible

The brand should feel human and approachable without becoming casual, gimmicky, or vague.

### Scalable by default

Patterns should work across landing pages, product surfaces, marketing content, and future front-end builds.

### Accessible as a baseline

Accessibility should not be treated as polish. Color contrast, keyboard states, readable type, and clear hierarchy are part of the core system.

## Visual posture

- Clean
- Confident
- Modern
- Approachable
- Structured
- Lightly expressive
